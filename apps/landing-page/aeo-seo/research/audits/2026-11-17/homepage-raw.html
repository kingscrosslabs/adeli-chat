Why we built LedaChat

I ran my Instagram on ManyChat, the biggest name in comment to DM. I upgraded to Pro at $29 a month. Then a bill for over $100 showed up.

ManyChat prices by active contacts. The more people comment, the more you pay.

Go viral, and your bill goes viral with you.

I also wanted an MCP. Every reel I post comes with a guide, and I wanted to set up each one by asking my AI agent, not by clicking through a flow builder.

So we built Adeli, an API for ads and social media, and used it to solve our own problem. LedaChat is the ManyChat replacement I wanted: one flat price, with an MCP built in.

Mika Reyes, co-founder

FAQ

Is this allowed by Instagram?
Yes. LedaChat uses Instagram's official API through Adeli, a Meta Tech Provider, and only messages people who commented or replied to your story first.
What account do I need?
An Instagram professional account (Business or Creator). Switching is free in Instagram's settings.
Do I need an Adeli account?
Not for the hosted version. To self-host, you need an Adeli API key, and Adeli's free plan covers your own account.
What happens at the free limit?
Your 10 live automations keep running. Pause one to start another.
Does it work on Facebook or TikTok?
Right now, it works on Instagram only. If you want it on Facebook or TikTok, send us a feature request at hello@ledachat.com.
Why use LedaChat over ManyChat?
A couple of reasons:
  • We offer an MCP, so you can ask Claude, ChatGPT or others to set up your automations for you. Better yet, turn them into skills.
  • We do not charge by active contact, which racks up your bill when a post goes viral.
  • We offer very simple, targeted features.
Can I cancel Pro?
Yes. It's monthly, and you can cancel anytime.
