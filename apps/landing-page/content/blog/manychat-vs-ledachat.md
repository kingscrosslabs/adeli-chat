I'm biased here: I used ManyChat, got a bill I didn't expect, and then we built LedaChat. So instead of telling you LedaChat wins, I'll show you where each one is the better pick. For most creators who only use comment to DM, it's LedaChat. For anyone running real chatbots across channels, it's still ManyChat.

## How do ManyChat and LedaChat compare?

| | LedaChat | ManyChat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Free for 10 live automations, then $25 a month | Pro from $39 a month, or less billed yearly |
| Does the price rise with reach? | No. One flat price whether 50 or 50,000 people comment | Yes. It rises with your active contacts |
| Runs on Meta's official APIs | Yes, through Adeli, an official Meta Tech Provider | Yes |
| Comment to DM on Instagram | Yes | Yes |
| Story replies | Yes | Yes |
| Follow check before the link | Yes | Yes |
| Email capture | Yes | Yes |
| Built-in MCP for Claude and ChatGPT | Yes | No |
| Open source and self-hostable | Yes, MIT license | No |
| Visual flow builder | No | Yes |
| Messenger, WhatsApp, SMS, email | No, Instagram only | Yes |
| Broadcasts and sequences | No | Yes |

ManyChat prices are from November 2026, after ManyChat changed its model in March 2026. Check their pricing page for your account, since older accounts can be on legacy plans.

## Why does ManyChat get expensive?

ManyChat bills by active contacts: anyone who interacted with your account in the billing period. Comment to DM creates contacts fast, because every person who comments your keyword becomes one. That's the point of the automation, and it's also what moves you up the price tiers.

That's how my $29 plan turned into a bill over $100. Nothing was broken. The reel just did well. LedaChat doesn't count contacts at all. Free is limited by how many automations are live at once, and Pro is unlimited at one price.

## What can LedaChat do that ManyChat can't?

Two things.

**Your AI can build the automations.** LedaChat has an MCP server built in, so Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or Claude Code can create an automation from a sentence like "when someone comments GUIDE on my latest reel, DM them my guide link." It makes a draft, shows you, and goes live when you say so. ManyChat's API can't create flows or keyword triggers, so no ManyChat MCP can do this. [Is there a ManyChat MCP?](/blog/manychat-mcp) explains why.

**You can own it.** LedaChat is MIT licensed. You can read the code, host it on your own Vercel account for free, and keep your data there.

## What can ManyChat do that LedaChat can't?

More than I'd like, if you need it:

- **A full flow builder.** Branching conversations, conditions, quizzes, multi-step sequences. LedaChat runs one fixed flow: reply, DM with a button, optional follow check and email, then your links.
- **Other channels.** Messenger, WhatsApp, SMS and email. LedaChat is Instagram only for now.
- **Broadcasts.** Messaging your contact list later. LedaChat only messages people inside the flow they triggered.
- **Integrations and a mature ecosystem.** Templates, agencies and years of tutorials.

If any of those are central to how you sell, LedaChat isn't a replacement.

## How does the comment to DM flow differ?

The core is the same in both, and both run on Meta's official APIs. LedaChat connects through [Adeli](https://www.tryadeli.com/?utm_source=ledachat.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ledachat-blog&utm_content=manychat-vs-ledachat), an official Meta Tech Provider, so it never asks for your password or uses browser bots. Someone comments a keyword, gets a public reply, then gets a DM. Instagram only allows one message until they respond, so both tools send a DM with a button first, and the link after the tap.

In LedaChat, each automation is the same short form: a trigger (one post, your next post, every post, or story replies), keywords or any comment, public reply variations, the opening DM and its button, an optional follow check and email ask, and a final DM with up to three links. You can't build a maze, which is the point. Most people's ManyChat comment automations already look like this.

LedaChat also has two things worth knowing when you switch. **Message earlier comments** sends the flow to people who commented in the last 24 hours before you went live (a Pro feature). **Audience** shows who engages the most across your automations.

## How do I switch from ManyChat to LedaChat?

There's no import, but each automation takes a couple of minutes:

1. List your ManyChat keyword automations: post, keyword, reply, DM text and link.
2. Rebuild each one in LedaChat, or describe it to Claude and let it make the drafts. The [setup guide](/blog/manychat-mcp-claude) shows how to connect it.
3. Pause the ManyChat version before you take the LedaChat one live, so nobody gets two DMs.
4. Test by commenting from a second account.
