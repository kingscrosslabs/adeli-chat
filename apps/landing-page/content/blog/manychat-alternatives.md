I ran my Instagram on ManyChat for a while. I upgraded to Pro at $29 a month, and then a bill for over $100 showed up, because ManyChat prices by active contacts. Every viral reel made my bill go viral too.

This list is for people who mostly use ManyChat for one thing: when someone comments a keyword on a post, send them a DM with a link. If you run complex multichannel chatbots, ManyChat is still the most complete tool, and some of these alternatives won't fit.

## Why are people looking for a ManyChat alternative?

Three reasons come up again and again:

- **The bill grows with your reach.** ManyChat charges by active contacts. Since March 2026, Pro starts at $39 a month for a small contact allowance, and every extra contact costs more. One viral post can multiply your bill.
- **It's a lot of tool for one job.** The flow builder is powerful, but if all you want is comment to DM, you're paying for and clicking through features you don't use.
- **AI can't set it up for you.** ManyChat has no official MCP server, and its API can't create flows or keyword triggers. I wrote about that in [Is there a ManyChat MCP?](/blog/manychat-mcp).

## 1. LedaChat: open source, free and with an MCP

LedaChat is an open-source comment to DM app for Instagram, with an MCP built in, running on Meta's official APIs.

**Official Meta APIs:** LedaChat connects to Instagram through [Adeli](https://www.tryadeli.com/?utm_source=ledachat.com&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=ledachat-blog&utm_content=manychat-alternatives), an official Meta Tech Provider. It never asks for your Instagram password, doesn't scrape and doesn't use browser bots.

**Open source:** the code is MIT licensed. You can read it, host it on your own account and keep your data there.

**Free:** two ways. Self-host it and it's free forever, with unlimited automations. Or use the hosted version, which is free for 10 live automations with every feature, and $25 a month for unlimited. Neither price moves with your reach, whether 50 or 50,000 people comment.

**With an MCP:** Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor can create, launch and pause automations from a chat, no clicking through a builder. The [setup guide](/blog/manychat-mcp-claude) shows how.

**What it does:** keyword or any-comment triggers on one post, your next post or every post, plus story replies. Each automation can post a public reply with random variations, send a DM with a button, check that the person follows you, ask for their email, and send a final DM with up to three links. Each person gets the flow once.

**The limits:** Instagram only for now, and comment to DM and story replies, not a full chatbot builder. The hosted version is opening soon, so you join a waitlist first. Self-hosting works today.

## 2. LinkDM: best for low volume on a budget

LinkDM is a focused Instagram DM tool. The free tier covers about 1,000 DMs a month on one account, and Pro is around $19 a month for a much higher DM cap across a few accounts.

It's simple to set up and cheap at small volume. The catch is that it's priced by DMs sent, so like ManyChat, a viral post pushes you toward the next tier. Some features, like comment auto-replies, sit on the paid plan.

## 3. CreatorFlow: best for creators who want tiers by volume

CreatorFlow is another Instagram-first DM tool with a flat monthly price per tier. The free plan includes around 500 DMs a month, and paid plans start near $15 a month, with higher tiers for more DMs and more workspaces.

It's a good fit if you can predict your DM volume. Reviews say automations pause when you hit your cap rather than billing you more, which is safer than surprise overages but means a viral post can stop sending until you upgrade.

## 4. InstantDM: best for unlimited contacts at a low price

InstantDM's single-account plans start around $12 a month (less billed yearly) with unlimited contacts. Instead of charging by volume, it caps how many DMs go out per hour.

That makes the bill predictable. The trade-off is speed during a spike: if thousands of people comment at once, some wait in a queue. Sources disagree on its exact tiers and caps, so read its pricing page closely.

## 5. Inro: best if you want AI replies inside the DMs

Inro is an Instagram DM platform with AI features on top of comment to DM. It has a free plan for about 100 active contacts, and paid plans start around €13 a month.

Like ManyChat, it's priced by active contacts, so it gets more expensive as you grow. It's a fit if you want AI-written replies inside conversations, not just a keyword and a link.

## 6. Chatfuel: best if you need more than Instagram

Chatfuel is a chatbot builder for Instagram, Facebook Messenger and WhatsApp, closer to ManyChat in scope than the others here. It has AI features and a visual builder.

Its pricing changed several times in 2026, and recent reviews quote figures from about $15 to $69 a month depending on the plan and date. Check the live pricing page. Pick it if you need several channels, not just Instagram comment to DM.
